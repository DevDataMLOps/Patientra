# Human oversight placeholder

Future uncertain patient links must be reviewed in an authenticated, least-privilege
queue that shows only the minimum evidence required. Reviewers need training, written
decision criteria, an escalation route, abstain capability, dual review for especially
risky cases, and periodic quality sampling.

The system must record the case ID, evidence categories, decision, reviewer identity,
time, rule/model version, and later corrections without copying raw identifiers into
general logs. Reviewer disagreements and reversal rates should inform threshold
changes, but threshold changes require versioning and approval.

Phase 1 creates no match candidates and therefore no review outcomes.
